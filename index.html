My promise to you

In every mood, in every moment…

I want to be the person who comes closer—not only when life is easy, but especially when it isn't.

01 — WHEN YOU SLEEP

Let the world wait.

When you're sleepy, I'll come close, hold you gently in my arms, and let you rest knowing you're safe with me.

02 — WHEN YOU CRY

You don't have to hide your tears.

I'll come to you, hold you close, and stay beside you. You never need to pretend to be okay with me.

03 — WHEN YOU'RE HAPPY

Your laugh is my favourite sound.

I'll make silly faces, tease you sweetly, pull you into my arms—and laugh right along with you.

04 — OUR CUDDLE TIME

Our own little universe.

When you want cuddles, we'll tuck ourselves into our cosy little corner, hug close, and forget the noise outside.

05 — WHEN YOU PLAY WITH MY HAIR

Mess it up, love.

If playing with my hair makes you happy, it's all yours. I'll just sit there smiling because your hands are my favourite stylist.

06 — WHEN YOU FEEL SAD

Come here. I've got you.

You don't need the right words. I'll wrap my arms around you and give you room to feel whatever you feel.

07 — WHEN YOU'RE HUNGRY

One bite from my hand?

I'll feed you with my own hands, steal a little smile between bites, and make even an ordinary meal feel like a date.

08 — WHEN YOU'RE THIRSTY

Little acts, lots of love.

I'll bring you water and help you take a sip. I want my love to show up in the smallest, everyday things.

09 — WHEN YOU FEEL UNWANTED

I want all of you.

Your habits, your moods, your quirks, your quiet days, your loud days—I'm not asking you to be someone else. I choose the real you.

10 — 1 YEAR & 11 MONTHS

One cake. Two hearts. Us.

We'll cut our little anniversary cake, feed each other a bite, laugh together, and seal this moment with a kiss. And then—more memories.
